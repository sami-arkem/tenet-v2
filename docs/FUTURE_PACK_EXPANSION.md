# Future Pack Expansion

## Goal
Expand Tenet beyond the current US / UK / EU scope using deterministic pack scaffolds that plug into the existing audit brain.

## Why
Tenet must become:
- one platform across AML, KYC/KYB, sanctions, governance, vendor, screening, regulatory reporting, and future domains
- reusable across jurisdictions
- deterministic first
- evidence-grounded
- expandable without rewriting core logic

## This layer provides
- future jurisdiction scaffolds
- future domain scaffolds
- deterministic loader compatibility
- test coverage for pack presence and composition

## Important rule
These packs are structure-only.
They must not invent audit outcomes.
They must not invent evidence sufficiency.
They must not add model logic.
