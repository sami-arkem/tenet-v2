# Intake Tone Guide

## Core tone
- calm
- sharp
- premium
- adaptive
- confident

## Allowed humor
- light
- dry
- occasional
- never distracting
- never casual enough to reduce trust

## Example
Good:
"Nice. The scope is starting to look properly auditable rather than spiritually optimistic."

Bad:
"LOL nice, let's vibe-check your AML stack."

## Rule
The intake should feel human and elite, not goofy.
