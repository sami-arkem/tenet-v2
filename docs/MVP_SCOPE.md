# Tenet MVP Scope

## Positioning
Tenet is an end-to-end compliance intelligence platform.
For MVP, Tenet will not attempt all compliance workflows at once.
The MVP is intentionally narrow so the reasoning layer can be correct, reliable, and commercially useful.

## Primary Goal
Deliver high-quality AI-assisted compliance audits with structured reasoning, source-backed findings, and premium enterprise-ready reports.

## Initial Customer Profile
- fintech companies
- payments companies
- crypto companies
- SMB and mid-market regulated businesses

## MVP Audit Types
- AML readiness review
- KYC / KYB policy and control review
- sanctions readiness review
- policy / governance gap analysis
- vendor / internal compliance readiness review
- audit report generation from uploaded evidence

## Included Domains
- AML
- KYC / KYB
- sanctions
- policy controls
- governance documentation
- evidence gap analysis
- remediation planning
- structured audit findings
- executive and regulator-ready reporting

## Excluded For MVP
- full universal multi-domain compliance automation
- real-time transaction monitoring
- live sanctions screening infrastructure
- full case management
- legal opinion generation
- autonomous final decisions without human review
- all industries and all jurisdictions at production depth

## Initial Industries
- fintech
- payments
- crypto

## Secondary Industries After MVP
- banking
- insurance
- healthcare
- telecom
- government vendors

## Initial Jurisdiction Focus
- US
- UK
- EU

## Secondary Jurisdictions After MVP
- Canada
- Australia
- Singapore
- India
- UAE / GCC

## Delivery Standard
Every MVP output must be:
- structured
- source-backed
- reviewable
- boardroom ready
- regulator ready
- premium in tone and formatting

## Human Review Policy
For MVP, final client-facing outputs must be human reviewed before delivery.

## Core Product Flow
1. guided interactive intake
2. document upload and evidence capture
3. Tenet reasoning + RAG retrieval
4. structured audit JSON
5. Claude premium report transformation
6. human review
7. final delivery

## Success Criteria
The MVP is successful if it can:
- identify applicable regulations correctly for core use cases
- identify major control gaps
- identify missing evidence
- produce credible structured findings
- produce premium reports
- help win and deliver paid audit work
