# Tenet Audit Types

## AML Readiness Review

Purpose:
Assess whether AML controls, governance, monitoring, escalation, and supporting evidence appear adequate for readiness.

## KYC/KYB Policy and Control Review

Purpose:
Assess whether customer and business onboarding controls, verification logic, and ownership checks are documented and defensible.

## Sanctions Readiness Review

Purpose:
Assess whether sanctions screening, match handling, escalation, and governance controls are adequately defined.

## Policy/Governance Gap Analysis

Purpose:
Assess whether policy architecture, accountability, review structure, and governance controls are complete and operationally credible.

## Vendor/Internal Compliance Readiness Review

Purpose:
Assess whether internal functions or critical vendors meet expected compliance governance, oversight, and evidence standards.

## Audit Report Generation From Uploaded Evidence

Purpose:
Generate a structured enterprise audit output from provided evidence using the locked output schema and human review.
