# TENET VISION

Tenet is an end-to-end global compliance intelligence platform.

The Tenet model performs deep structured compliance reasoning across domains, jurisdictions, entities, documents, transactions, and business workflows.

Claude converts structured Tenet outputs into world-class enterprise compliance reports, findings, and remediation plans.

Tenet is built to serve every business type, every compliance niche, and every jurisdiction with world-class structured compliance intelligence and reporting.
