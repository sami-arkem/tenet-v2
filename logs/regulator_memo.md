# Regulator Memo: Report Live Entity

## Supervisory Decision
- Status: BLOCKED
- Supervisory Summary: Deployment is blocked due to missing critical and high-severity controls or insufficient confidence in the entity's AML controls evidence.

## Control Gaps
The following controls are currently missing or undocumented, posing significant regulatory risks: AML-001 (Enterprise AML policy approved and version controlled), AML-002 (Customer risk rating methodology defined), AML-003 (Transaction monitoring framework documented), GOV-001 (Compliance governance roles and escalation paths defined), KYB-001 (Beneficial ownership and business verification controls documented), KYC-001 (Customer identification procedures documented), and SAN-001 (Sanctions screening program defined).

## Required Follow-Up
The entity must provide documented evidence and remediation plans for all missing controls listed above. Immediate priority should be given to critical controls AML-003 and SAN-001, with near-term remediation for the others within 30 to 90 days. Continued supervisory engagement will validate the sufficiency and effectiveness of remediation efforts before any approval for deployment.
