## Internal Operations

## Constraint
Any future bot from this catalog must:
- stay outside deterministic audit verdicting
- feed lawful source candidates through the canonical source-acquisition gate
- respect robots, licensing, provenance, and contractual authorization
- be introduced as a separate bounded subsystem with tests before activation
